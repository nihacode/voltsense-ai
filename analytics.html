📘 EV Battery Terminology

Key technical metrics used in EV thermal management & electrochemistry.

SOC
State of Charge: Represents the remaining capacity of the battery pack expressed as a percentage of its current maximum available energy (82%).
SOH
State of Health: A measure of the battery's current condition and capacity retention relative to its original design specification (96%).
BTMS
Battery Thermal Management System: Active liquid cooling and heating circulation loop engineered to maintain cells within optimal temperature limits (20-40°C).
Thermal Runaway
Critical Overheating Condition: An uncontrollable self-heating chain reaction caused by internal shorting, overcharging, or severe mechanical damage.
