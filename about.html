About VoltSense AI

Platform Specifications, Architecture Inspiration, and Technical Documentation.

🏭 Industrial Dashboard Design

VoltSense AI is a next-generation conceptual user interface inspired by telemetry systems designed by industry leaders including Tesla, Rivian, Siemens, Bosch, ABB, and CATL.

Modern electric vehicle battery systems require continuous sub-second monitoring of cell thermals, voltage differentials, state of charge (SOC), and state of health (SOH). VoltSense AI provides engineers and fleet operators with a clean, industrial, high-contrast dashboard to detect thermal hotspots before runaway conditions develop.

Core Platform Capabilities
  • Sub-Module Thermography: Multi-point thermistor array modeling.
  • AI Degradation Forecasting: Machine-learning algorithms projecting battery lifespan.
  • BTMS Liquid Cooling Modulation: Automated coolant pump and valve triggers.
  • Standardized Fleet Service Schedules: Automated maintenance workflows.
