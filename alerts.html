📋 Maintenance Checklist

Standard operating procedures for EV battery servicing and inspection.

  1. Inspect Battery

    Perform visual physical check of high-voltage pack casing, protective underbody armor, and seals for damage or swelling.

  2. Check Cooling

    Inspect BTMS liquid glycol coolant levels, pump flow pressures, radiator fins, and thermal hose connections.

  3. Review Sensors

    Calibrate NTC thermistors, CAN-bus telemetry nodes, and current sensor shunts across all pack modules.

  4. Analyze Logs

    Extract BMS error codes, historical peak thermal cycles, voltage degradation logs, and AI predictive indicators.

  5. Generate Report

    Compile final battery State of Health (SOH) laboratory report and issue servicing sign-off certificate.
